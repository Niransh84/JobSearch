# Job Search Shortlist

Weekday-updated shortlist of pharmaceutical and medical-device quality, compliance, vigilance, complaint management, QMS, CAPA, labeling, and post-market surveillance roles in Illinois or US-remote. Each role meets a $100K pay floor and the list is sorted by fit.

Live report: https://niransh84.github.io/JobSearch/job_search_R.html

A second report, job_search_N.html, is live at https://niransh84.github.io/JobSearch/job_search_N.html

The report has three tables:

- Shortlist: roles at 70% fit or higher
- Notable roles under 70%
- Roles dropped, with the reason

Fit weights:

| Factor | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Required skills | 30 |
| Overall requirements | 25 |
| Experience | 20 |
| Industry | 10 |
| Title / seniority | 8 |
| Preferred skills | 7 |

The shortlist refreshes on weekdays at about 8 AM Central.

No personal or contact information is included.
